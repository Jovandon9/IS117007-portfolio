# Sources and research method

This site combines role explanations with practical learning guidance. The sources below support concepts, skills, and research methods. They do not establish how many jobs exist, what every employer requires, or what you will earn. No continuously maintained job board or salary survey is provided.

## Job-example maintenance policy

The [student job examples](/guide/remote-jobs) form a dated research sample. Each record displays its own last recorded check date and status. **Checked** means the original description and application route were observed on that date; it does not guarantee availability later. **Not reverified** means the recorded example has not been reopened for the latest update and must not be treated as a confirmed opening. A closed posting should be labeled historical when closure is observed, rather than silently described as current.

The original employer is the authority for current availability, location restrictions, qualifications and deadlines. State compatibility does not establish personal eligibility. Unknown location rules are labeled unknown.

[Report a closed or changed posting](https://github.com/Jovandon9/IS117007-portfolio/issues/new) with the title, original URL, date and observed change. Keep private career reflections and eligibility circumstances out of issues. Reports are reviewed manually; there is no promised refresh interval.

## Primary resources to consult

| Resource | What it helps you investigate | Important limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Microsoft Learn: Azure AI Engineer Associate](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-ai-engineer/) | Examples of applied AI implementation skills and solution responsibilities | A vendor credential describes its own ecosystem; it is not a universal hiring requirement |
| [Google Cloud: Professional Machine Learning Engineer](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/machine-learning-engineer) | Data, model, production, and monitoring responsibilities | A certification outline does not prove an employer requires certification |
| [Palantir careers](https://www.palantir.com/careers/) | An employer resource for researching forward-deployed engineering terminology and responsibilities | Listings change; forward deployment varies by employer and is not always an AI role |
| [OpenAI: Evaluation best practices](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices) | Evaluation design, task-specific examples, and iterative measurement | Tool documentation describes a practice, not an occupation's entire scope |
| [Hugging Face: LLM Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter1/1) | Language-model concepts and hands-on technical learning | Completing a course is not equivalent to production experience |
| [Google PAIR: People + AI Guidebook](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook/) | User needs, feedback, expectations, and human-centered AI product design | Product guidance must be adapted to the actual users and context |
| [NIST: AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) | Risk vocabulary, governance, measurement, and management | A framework is not legal advice or a certification of safety |

External pages may move or change. Check their current content and publication information before relying on a specific requirement. These are learning and methodology references, not a claim that the linked organizations are currently hiring for the profiles on this site.

## Keep a dated job-description worksheet

Use one row per actual employer posting:

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Employer, exact job title, and original careers URL |
| Timing | Posting date if given; your access date; whether it still accepts applications |
| Context | Location, remote restrictions, seniority, and relevant team |
| Responsibilities | Three concrete outputs or recurring activities |
| Requirements | Minimum qualifications separately from preferred experience |
| Constraints | Travel, on-call work, work authorization, or other stated conditions |
| Your interpretation | Likely role family, evidence for that choice, and uncertainties |

Avoid assuming the displayed posting date is the day a role was first created. If a date is missing, record “not provided.” If you cannot verify that a job is still open, record that uncertainty.

## Separate observations from conclusions

An observation might be: “Four of the six postings I reviewed asked for Python.” A careful conclusion is: “Python is worth prioritizing for this sample.” It would be unsupported to claim that the entire AI industry requires Python based on that sample alone.

For compensation research, record currency, location, level, base salary versus total compensation, and the source date. Compare like with like. This site intentionally avoids a single salary number for each title because those conditions differ substantially.

Revisit your worksheet when applying. Use the [getting-started guide](/guide/getting-started) to turn the evidence into your next learning or portfolio decision.
