# AI solutions architect

**Design an AI system that fits its business, security, and operational constraints.** A solutions architect connects technical choices with the needs of users, engineering teams, and decision-makers. AI architecture adds model uncertainty, data permissions, evaluation, and inference cost to familiar system-design concerns.

This is often an experienced role. Some positions support sales or customer adoption; others sit inside a product or enterprise architecture team. The balance of design, prototypes, implementation, and stakeholder communication varies by employer.

## What the work looks like

Before choosing a model or framework, an architect clarifies the problem, the acceptable error rate, the data involved, and the systems that need to integrate. They compare options such as retrieval, fine-tuning, a conventional rules engine, a managed service, or a self-hosted deployment.

Typical responsibilities include gathering requirements, designing data flows and service boundaries, identifying trust boundaries, estimating resource use, documenting tradeoffs, and reviewing operational plans. Architects may build prototypes to test a risky assumption rather than treating every design decision as settled.

Deliverables can include an architecture diagram, an architecture decision record, an implementation roadmap, a threat model, a cost estimate with stated assumptions, and a plan for evaluating quality and reliability. The best design is understandable to the team that must operate it.

## Skills to develop

- **System design:** APIs, distributed systems, storage, networking, and reliability patterns.
- **AI patterns:** retrieval, model hosting, fine-tuning tradeoffs, evaluations, and human escalation.
- **Security and governance:** identity, least privilege, data classification, retention, and auditability.
- **Communication:** clear diagrams, written decisions, stakeholder workshops, and explaining uncertainty.

Cloud certifications can organize learning, but they do not establish architectural judgment on their own. Practice comparing multiple feasible approaches with evidence.

## Illustrative job brief

::: info Example written for this atlas
Design an internal knowledge assistant for a company with several document systems. Identify permission boundaries, compare managed and self-hosted options, propose a retrieval architecture, and define a phased proof of concept. Document quality targets, operational owners, and the conditions required before a wider rollout.
:::

This describes a possible project, not a vacancy or a standard entry-level assignment.

## A realistic entry route

Build experience in software, platform engineering, cloud operations, security, or technical implementation. Practice producing design documents for systems you have actually built. Early-career roles such as cloud engineer or solutions engineer may provide exposure to architecture under supervision.

Because architecture decisions affect multiple teams, employers often look for experience delivering and operating systems. Treat the title as a direction to grow toward rather than assuming a short course makes every architecture role accessible.

## Portfolio proof

Write a design proposal for a public-data research assistant. Include requirements, a data-flow diagram, two plausible architectures, an explicit choice, and a small prototype testing the hardest assumption. Estimate cost using transparent workload assumptions; label it an estimate rather than a guaranteed bill. Explain failure recovery and who approves access.

## How this role differs

A [forward-deployed engineer](/roles/forward-deployed-engineer) commonly implements a customer-specific solution directly. An [AI engineer](/roles/ai-engineer) builds the application. The architect emphasizes how the complete system fits together and why its choices meet the requirements, though hands-on work may be substantial.
